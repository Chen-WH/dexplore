Inspire Hand URDF — License and Attribution
============================================

Source
------
The CAD model and URDF for this dexterous hand are derived from materials
provided by Inspire Robotics (https://www.inspire-robots.com/).

Modifications
-------------
This is a derivative work with the following modifications:
  - SolidWorks-based rebuild of the URDF and meshes
  - 6-DOF floating-base wrist chain prepended for simulation control
  - Decimated collision meshes for Isaac Gym physics performance
  - Fingertip frames added (massless, no collision or visual geometry)
    to expose fingertip 3D positions to the policy observation
